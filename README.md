# 🌍 Global Crisis AI — Intelligent Decision System for Humanitarian Response

## 🚀 Overview

Global Crisis AI is an advanced simulation and decision-support system designed to model large-scale humanitarian crises and recommend optimal intervention strategies.

The platform integrates artificial intelligence, resource modeling, and scenario simulation to evaluate how different response strategies impact affected populations across regions.

---

## 🧠 Key Features

### 🌐 Global Crisis Dashboard

* Identifies high-risk countries based on crisis severity
* Visualizes global stress distribution
* Highlights populations most at risk

### 🧪 Simulation Lab

* Models real-world crisis scenarios using dynamic inputs
* Predicts affected populations based on resource availability
* Simulates impact of food, water, and energy shortages

### ⚖️ Strategy Engine

* Compares multiple intervention strategies
* Evaluates trade-offs between immediate aid and long-term investment
* Recommends optimal actions to minimize human impact

### 🧠 AI Explanation Layer

* Explains *why* certain strategies are recommended
* Identifies key drivers of crisis risk
* Improves transparency in decision-making

---

## 🌍 Real-World Relevance

This system is designed with real global challenges in mind, including:

* Food insecurity across parts of Africa
* Flood and population pressures in South Asia
* Energy instability in developing regions

It reflects the type of tools used by humanitarian organizations and policy analysts to guide large-scale decisions.
